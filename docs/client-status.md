# MDW Wellness — Platform Status

_A plain-language summary of where your booking & operations platform stands._

**MDW Wellness** takes a customer from their first enquiry all the way to a paid, therapist-assigned visit — and bills it. It's your team's day-to-day workspace for running the service.

---

## Where things stand

The platform is **built and deployed**, and we're in **final end-to-end testing** before you begin using it with real customers. The complete journey works:

1. **A customer enquires** — from your website, or entered by your team.
2. **Your team reaches out** — logs the call, confirms what the customer needs.
3. **The booking is confirmed** — online consultation or home visit, with the fee filled in automatically.
4. **Payment is taken** — the customer gets a payment link; your team confirms it's received.
5. **A therapist is assigned** — only *after* payment — and the booking moves onto the appointments list.
6. **An invoice is raised** automatically.

Every customer, booking, payment, and invoice is recorded, with a full history of who did what.

---

## What your team can do today

- Capture and track enquiries from the website and by phone
- See who still needs a first call, and who's already being handled
- Confirm bookings and take payment **before** a therapist is committed
- See at a glance which therapists are free on a given day and time
- Manage your services and pricing
- Raise and manage invoices
- Manage staff accounts and their roles

---

## How payments work right now

A customer pays by **UPI** through a link your team sends them. **Your team confirms the money has arrived and marks it received** — and only then does the system unlock the therapist assignment. This keeps you in control of every payment before a therapist is committed to a visit.

_(Automatic payment confirmation — so your team doesn't check each one by hand — is planned for when your booking volume grows.)_

Once those are in place, the payment link is live for customers to pay.
